Home farm switcher

Interactive mock. Tap the farm name in the header.

  1. The sheet lists only crops added on the Crops page. Empty farms never appear.
  2. Each row: crop name, farm name, day count, soil status, and harvest date.
  3. Picking a crop closes the sheet, and all of Home switches to it: header, crop progress, soil readings, AI advice, and weather.
  4. "Add a crop" opens the Crops page.

Mock data only. Not app code.
